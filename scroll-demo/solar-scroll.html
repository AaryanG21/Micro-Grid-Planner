Dropping in the generated clip

The scene is pure SVG, so the page runs with no assets. To put a rendered clip behind it, set CLIP_URL at the top of the script to an mp4 and the video is scrubbed by scroll position rather than played, which is what makes it feel welded to the page.

Encode the clip with dense keyframes or seeking will stutter: ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -g 1 -c:v libx264 -crf 22 -an out.mp4

Figures are illustrative: 24 modules at 550 W, 4.9 peak sun hours, 0.78 performance ratio. Wire the readouts to the real sizing endpoint to make them true.
