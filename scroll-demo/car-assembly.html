    How it is put together

    Twelve groups in one inline SVG. Each has a fly-in vector, a rotation, and a scroll window. Within its window a part translates home, draws its outline with stroke-dashoffset, then fades fill in over the last 40 percent, which is what reads as curing rather than appearing.

    To retarget this to your own subject, replace the twelve path groups and edit the PARTS array. The timing, mass and downforce columns are data, not code, so a different machine is a different table.

    Silhouette is a generic open-wheel car, not any team car. Mass 798 kg and 1400 kg of downforce are plausible order-of-magnitude figures used to make the counters mean something.
